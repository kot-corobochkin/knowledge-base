Instrumental use of individuals

## 1. **Simple definition (what is it?)**

👉 **Instrumental use of individuals is when people are used as a means to achieve a goal, rather than valued for themselves.**

---

## 2. **Core mechanism (how does it work?)**

👉 **A group or authority identifies a goal → assigns roles to people → evaluates them based on usefulness, not personality.**

---

## 3. **Why it happens (motivation)**

👉 **It happens because organizations prioritize efficiency, control, and outcomes over individual autonomy.**

---

## 4. **Example (from real life / text)**

👉 **In the text, party leaders use members to gain influence in other organizations, controlling how far they can rise.**

---

## 5. **Effect (what are the consequences?)**

👉

- individuals feel replaceable
- loyalty is demanded
- personal beliefs may be suppressed

---

## 6. **Contrast (what it is NOT)**

👉 **It is not about personal growth or mutual respect — it is about utility.**

---

## 7. **One-line summary (punchline)**

👉 **People become tools, not ends.**
